# Partnership opportunities for Gini Health

Prepared 29 October 2026 with Partner Finder, starting from **gini.health**. **28 companies** in **6 categories**, each taken from a live web search result and linked to that source.

## How Gini was read

**In one line:** Gini offers needle-free, at-home blood testing for women, timed to the menstrual cycle, with doctor-reviewed results and clear next steps.

**Product:** A pre-launch kit of two at-home, needle-free blood collections (FDA-cleared device, CLIA/CAP lab) timed to the user's cycle. It covers a panel of about 24 markers, including reproductive hormones, thyroid, HbA1c, lipids/ApoB, ferritin, hsCRP, kidney/liver and vitamin D. Results are interpreted alongside cycle and symptoms, doctor-reviewed, and paired with guidance on what to discuss with a physician.

**Customers:** Consumer women (direct-to-consumer, HSA/FSA eligible) with symptoms such as fatigue, weight gain, irregular periods, acne/hirsutism, hair thinning or hot flashes. Named segments include women exploring PCOS/PMOS, women in perimenopause, women on GLP-1s, and women wanting a health baseline. The waitlist has 5,000+ sign-ups.

**Value proposition:** Cycle-aware timing with two draws gives hormone results proper context, which single-draw tests lack. It is convenient and needle-free at home, with physician-reviewed, plain-language interpretation and next steps.

**Geography:** Likely United States (HSA/FSA, CLIA, HIPAA, FDA references); not explicitly stated

**Gaps that partners could fill:**

- Clinical care/telehealth partner to diagnose, prescribe and treat (e.g., PCOS, perimenopause HRT) beyond 'wellness insights'
- Distribution via employers, women's health benefits platforms and payers, since the company is pre-launch, DTC and waitlist-only
- Referral channels from OB-GYNs, fertility clinics and GLP-1/weight-management programs
- Integrations with cycle-tracking apps and wearables for cycle timing and symptom data
- Brand trust and reach beyond founder Instagram (@doctor.kenza) via established women's health brands
